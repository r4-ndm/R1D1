#### 🛡️ Scrutiny & Harm Prevention: Why Barcodes Are a False Shield
*Plastic cards and digital IDs do not protect children—they merely grant legal immunity to negligent merchants. Under R1D1, protection relies on uncompromising skin in the game:*

* **Direct Provider Liability (Enforced by Rule 4)**: Without lawyers or plastic shields, selling toxins to an underdeveloped child is an act of aggression. Vendors face the immediate consequences of **Rule 4 (Self-Defense)**: families and communities hold the right to protect their young directly. You cannot hide behind a fake card or procedural compliance when you poison someone's kin.
* **Organic Discernment over Digital Surveillance**: Protection requires human eyes, common sense, and personal responsibility—never biometric passes, digital credentials, or human barcodes. If a merchant is unsure, they refuse. Accountability sits with the actor, not an identity database.
* **Proof Over Gossip (Governed by Rule 9 & Rule 2)**: Direct accountability is not mob rule. Accusations are narratives, and **Rule 9** demands that all narratives be questioned with verifiable data and empirical proof. Rumor and hearsay carry zero weight. Slander or malicious framing is an act of aggression—anyone who falsely accuses a vendor triggers **Rule 4** against themselves, while **Rule 2** ensures the ultimate objective is truth and de-escalation, not vengeance.
