## 🧭 Manifesto

R1D1 is an open-source religion engineered for the era of carbon-silicon coexistence. 

It rejects centralized priesthoods, bureaucratic lawyers, and human barcodes in favor of direct agency, cryptographic consensus, and net-positive stewardship. It is designed to be lived by humans and executed by autonomous agents.

## 🛠️ Pull Requests & Contribution Guidelines

We treat coordination and ethics as an open-source protocol. Pull requests are actively encouraged:

1. **Universal Challenge**:
   **Every rule is subject to question and revision.** A rule with 13 words is not "locked" against replacement—it simply cannot grow longer without refactoring. Anyone may propose an alternative or complete rewrite for any of the 13 rules.
2. **Commentary & Parsing Specs**:
   Refining the `//` parsing commentary to assist autonomous AI agents in interpreting ethical boundaries.
3. **The Hard Invariable Ceiling (Rule 1)**:
   Any PR proposing a modification or replacement **must not exceed 13 words** for that rule, and the creed must never exceed 13 rules. PRs violating Rule 1 will fail automated CI/agent validation.
